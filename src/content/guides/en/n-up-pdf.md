Printing dozens of lecture slides one per page uses a lot of paper. Putting 2 or 4 pages on each sheet cuts that to a half or a quarter. Printers have a "pages per sheet" setting too, but saving the result as a PDF lets you send it to others or print it as is at a copy shop.

## Steps

1. Open [N-up PDF](/en/pdf/n-up/).
2. Click "Choose PDF file" or drag a PDF onto the screen.
3. Choose how many pages go on each sheet (2, 4, 6 or 9) and the paper size (A4 or Letter).
4. Check the first sheet preview.
5. Click "Create N-up PDF", then "Download" when it is done.

## How many pages per sheet?

| Per sheet | Orientation | Good for                                       |
| --------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 2         | Landscape   | Text-heavy documents, book-like reading        |
| 4         | Portrait    | Lecture slides and presentations (most common) |
| 6         | Landscape   | Slides that are mostly pictures                |
| 9         | Portrait    | Skimming the overall flow at a glance          |

The orientation is chosen for you. It gives the largest cells for portrait documents.

Printed text shrinks along with the pages. With 4 pages on a sheet, text is about half its original size; with 9, about a third, so small text gets hard to read. Start with 4.

## Borders

"Border around each page" draws a thin gray line around each page. When white pages sit side by side it can be hard to see where one ends, and the line makes that clear.

## Fix the page order first

Pages are placed in their current order, left to right and top to bottom. To drop a cover or blank page or change the order, use [Organize PDF](/en/pdf/organize/) first. To combine several files, [merge them](/en/pdf/merge/) first.

## Common questions

**Will the text get blurry?** No. Pages are scaled down as they are, not turned into images. They stay sharp when zoomed in, and text can still be selected and searched.

**What if the pages are different sizes?** Each page is scaled to fit its cell, keeping its proportions, and centered. Portrait and landscape pages can be mixed. Rotated pages are placed the way they appear on screen.

**Can I print double-sided?** Yes, just print the PDF double-sided. With 4 pages per sheet on both sides, one sheet of paper holds 8 pages.

## Why isn't anything uploaded?

Scaling and arranging pages is quick work for a modern browser. There is no need to send lecture notes or work documents to a server, and your file never leaves your device.
